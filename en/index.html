The learning path

From “What is a program?” to “I build classes with inheritance.” Best done in order, but skipping around is allowed.

The time shown covers the material: reading, thinking along, solving exercises.

  1. 1

    Chapter 1: What is programming, anyway?

    Compiler versus interpreter, and why Java is strict enough to help you.

     35 min

    Chapter 2: Your first program

    Class, main, println and a search game: find errors before the compiler reports them.

     40 min
  3. 3

    Chapter 3: Variables & data types

     50 min
  4. 4

     45 min

How this works

Think first, then click

Before many experiments you make a prediction first: if you guess first, you learn twice as much from watching.

Spot optional content at once

Cards with a flag and the optional tag open in their own window: level-ups, legendary exercises, other explanations. None of it is tested.
